ABOUT THE RESORT

Where nature slows you down

Nestled in the serene hills of Tarebhir, Tranquility Paradise Resort offers a peaceful escape from the city — just moments from Kathmandu's cultural heart. Here, mornings begin with birdsong and mountain air, and evenings unfold at their own unhurried pace.
