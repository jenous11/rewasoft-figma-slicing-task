About Tranquility Paradise

Set in the serene surroundings of Tarebhir, Tranquility Paradise Resort brings together nature, comfort and genuine hospitality in a setting made for escaping the everyday.

OUR STORY

A little away from everything

Tranquility Paradise Resort was born from a simple idea — that the world moves too quickly, and some moments deserve to be unhurried. Tucked into the hillside of Tarebhir, the resort sits close enough to Kathmandu to reach easily, yet far enough to let the noise of the city fade entirely.

Here, the surrounding landscape does much of the work. Mornings begin with open air and birdsong. Evenings gather people together around shared meals, conversation and quiet reflection. The resort is designed not just as a place to sleep, but as a space to slow down, reconnect and create something worth remembering.

Location

TAREBHIR HILLSIDE

Perched in the peaceful hills of Tarebhir, Gokarneshwor, the resort enjoys a setting where nature surrounds you on every side. The Kathmandu valley and its energy remain reachable — but at the resort, that distance feels much greater. Trees, open sky and the rhythm of the hillside replace the usual backdrop of the city.
